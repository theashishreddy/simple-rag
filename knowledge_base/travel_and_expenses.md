# ACME Global - Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Flight and Transportation Bookings
- **Advance Booking**: All flight bookings should be made at least 14 days in advance through Navan (corporate travel portal).
- **Cabin Class Policy**:
  - Flights under 6 hours: Economy or Premium Economy class only.
  - Flights over 6 continuous hours (transcontinental or international): Business class is permitted with manager approval.
- **Ground Transit**: Trains, airport shuttles, rideshares (Uber/Lyft), and taxis are eligible for reimbursement. Rental cars are permitted when public transport or rideshare is impractical.

## 2. Daily Per Diem & Meals
- **Domestic Travel (US & Canada)**: Up to \$85 per full day for meals (breakfast \$20, lunch \$25, dinner \$40).
- **International Travel**: Up to \$110 per full day.
- **Alcohol**: Alcoholic beverages consumed during individual travel are not reimbursable. Team dinners or client entertainment meals allow moderate alcohol with Director approval.

## 3. Expense Submission Deadlines & Receipts
- **Filing Window**: All travel and business expenses must be submitted within 30 calendar days following the conclusion of the trip.
- **Receipts**: Itemized digital receipts are mandatory for all transactions exceeding \$25 USD.
- **Payment Method**: Corporate credit cards are issued to employees who travel more than twice annually. Personal cards may be used and reimbursed within two pay cycles.
