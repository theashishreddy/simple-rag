# ACME Global - Company Policies & Benefits

## 1. Remote and Flexible Work Policy
Employees at ACME Global enjoy a flexible hybrid working model.
- **Core Working Hours**: All teams must be reachable during core collaboration hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time.
- **Remote Days**: Team members can work up to 3 days remotely per week. Full-remote exceptions require approval from department heads.
- **Home Office Stipend**: Every full-time employee receives a one-time \$1,000 home office setup stipend upon joining, plus a recurring \$300 annual ergonomics refresh allowance.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leaves
- **Vacation Time**: Full-time employees accrue 25 paid vacation days per calendar year.
- **Rollover**: Up to 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over to the following year. Unused rollover days expire on March 31st.
- **Wellness Days**: ACME observes 4 company-wide "Recharge Days" each quarter, granting an extra paid day off for physical and mental rest.
- **Parental Leave**: 16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave for primary caregivers and 8 weeks for secondary caregivers, eligible within 12 months of birth or adoption.

## 3. Health & Wellness Program
- **Medical, Dental, and Vision**: 100% employer-covered premiums for employees and 80% coverage for dependents.
- **Mental Health Support**: Employees have free, confidential access to 12 therapy sessions per calendar year via ModernHealth.
- **Fitness Subsidy**: A \$75 monthly reimbursement is available for gym memberships, fitness trackers, or personal training subscriptions.
